# PaperBite Icon Prompt

Create a clean app icon for "PaperBite", a local paper-reading vault whose idea is "一口一篇论文": one concise bite per research paper.

Visual concept:
- A compact Markdown document shaped like a paper sheet.
- A small bite mark on the top-right corner of the sheet.
- Subtle folded-corner detail or short horizontal text lines to imply distilled notes.
- A small bookmark tab or index marker to suggest a paper knowledge base.

Style:
- Modern vector icon, flat with light dimensional shading.
- Works at 16px, 32px, 128px, and 512px.
- Rounded but not cartoonish.
- Crisp silhouette, high contrast, no tiny text.
- Background can be transparent or a simple rounded square.

Palette:
- Paper white or warm off-white for the sheet.
- Deep ink blue or charcoal for lines.
- Fresh accent green for the bite/bookmark.
- Optional small amber highlight, but keep the palette restrained.

Avoid:
- Literal teeth, mouths, faces, animals, mascots, or food imagery.
- Dense paragraphs, unreadable letters, conference logos, or publisher marks.
- Purple-blue gradient app icon cliches.

Output:
- Square icon, centered, vector-friendly.
- Provide SVG or high-resolution PNG.
